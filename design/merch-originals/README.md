# Squish Squad Shop — original concept sheets

The 26 merchandise concept sheets (1536×1024 JPEG) from the "Squish man Merch
zip" folder in Google Drive, unedited. They replace the 9 PNGs recovered from
the earlier truncated upload. They are kept here as the source of truth and are
**not** served by the site.

The web copies in `public/assets/merch/` are cropped to the product artwork
(the construction/specification, sample-review and supplier-approval panels
are removed) and re-encoded as WebP at 1400px and 720px wide. The two plush
sheets also yield a `*-detail-views` crop.
