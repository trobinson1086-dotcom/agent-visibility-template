PARENTS • TEACHERS • LIBRARIANS

PARENTS & TEACHERS RESOURCE CENTER

Turn each Squish Man adventure into a read-aloud, discussion, reflection or classroom activity. The series supports conversations about kindness, courage, confidence, persistence, patience, creativity, teamwork and being yourself.

📖Read-Aloud ReadyUse the stories for shared reading, morning meetings, library story time or family reading.
💬Discussion PromptsAsk what Squish Man tried, how the characters felt, what changed and what the reader might do next.
✏️Reflect & CreatePair stories with drawing, writing, coloring, problem-solving and the learning/activity editions.
🌎English + EspañolUse the growing English and Spanish collections with families and multilingual learning communities.

Book 1 • Kindness & Helping

Talk about noticing when someone needs help, communicating with friends and making a positive difference.

Try it:Ask: “What is one kind thing you can do for someone today?”

Book 2 • Believe in Yourself

Explore courage, persistence and how small steps can move a big dream forward.

Try it:Have children draw a big dream and write one small step they can take.

Book 3 • Patience & Caring

Use the Magical Garden story to discuss patience, caring for living things and why growth takes time.

Try it:Ask children to name something that becomes better when we care for it patiently.

Book 4 • Creativity & Teamwork

Discuss how friends can combine ideas, divide a big job into smaller jobs and solve problems together.

Try it:Give a small group one classroom problem and let each child contribute one possible solution.

Book 5 • Friendship & Being Yourself

Use the Maple Hollow Derby to talk about friendship, teamwork, trying your best and celebrating different strengths.

Try it:Ask: “What is something you do in your own special way?”

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What ages are Squish Man books for?

The series is designed primarily for K–4 readers, approximately ages 5–9.

Are books available in Spanish?

Yes. The storefront includes Spanish storybooks and Spanish learning/activity editions.

Can I preview a book before buying?

Yes. Books 1–5 now include real Look Inside sample pages taken from the approved interiors.

Do you offer school or bulk orders?

Schools, libraries and programs can contact schools@squishman.com.

Where can I get order help?

Email orders@squishman.com for order questions or support@squishman.com for customer support.

When can I buy Squish Man merchandise?

The Squish Squad Shop designs are coming soon. Prices, sizes and ordering will be announced at launch — email squishfans@squishman.com for a launch alert.

Are all checkout buttons live?

Yes. The current book listings are connected to their approved checkout links. Squish Squad Shop merchandise is coming soon and is not yet available to order.
