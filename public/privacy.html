Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

SquishMan.com (“Squish Man,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy and the privacy of your family. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Order information: when you buy something, we receive your name, email address, shipping address, phone number (if you provide one) and what you ordered.
  • Squish Squad membership: when a parent or guardian joins, we receive their name, email address and the membership plan, plus the child’s first name and birthday month for the member card and birthday gifts. Hero and Platinum members also give a mailing address for printed gifts, and Platinum members a T-shirt size for the birthday box. We never ask for a child’s last name, full birth date, email address or photo.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes basic technical data (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • Visit counts: our site counts its own visits so we know which pages and links people find useful. For each page view it records the page, the website or app the visit came from, browser, device type, language and an approximate location (country, state and city, from your internet provider). It does not use cookies, does not store your IP address and cannot follow you from one day to the next. Visit records are kept for up to 13 months and are not shared with anyone.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

How we use information

We use information to process and deliver your orders, send order and shipping updates, answer your questions, prevent fraud, keep required business and tax records, and send updates you have asked for.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the store, and only what they need:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Printful — printing and shipping Squish Squad Shop merchandise (your name, shipping address, email and phone, if provided).
  • Lulu — printing and shipping books.
  • Resend — sending order emails and Squish Squad Pack emails.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security.
  • Shipping carriers — delivering your package.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Children’s privacy

Squish Man books are made for children, but our store, email updates and contact options are for parents, guardians, teachers and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, other than the child’s first name and birthday month that a parent or guardian gives us for a Squish Squad membership. A parent or guardian can ask us to change or delete it at any time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email contact@squishman.com and we will delete it.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site may store small preferences in your browser, such as your language choice and whether you have visited before (a simple yes or no, not an identifier). Stripe’s checkout pages use their own cookies to process payments securely.

How long we keep information

We keep order records for as long as needed to provide customer support and meet tax and accounting requirements, then delete them. We keep membership details while the membership is active, and a parent or guardian can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you updates, by emailing contact@squishman.com. Some records may need to be kept to meet legal requirements.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact@squishman.com.
