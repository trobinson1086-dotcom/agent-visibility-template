(which says Pudy has a blue collar and calls Pudy "he") and the website's character captions.

## What this rules out in the existing footage
- Book 1: C03/C04 (golden "Peanut" puppy) and C05, C07, C10, C11, C15 and C25–C32 (dark-brown puppy) are off-model. Don't use them.
- Book 2: every clip with Pudy (C47, C50–C65, C86, C90) shows a **blue** collar. These need to be regenerated with a pink collar. A colour fix in editing isn't safe: changing blue to pink would also turn Squish Man's royal-blue cape pink.
- The theme-song artwork also shows Pudy with a blue collar. It's fine as audio, but avoid showing that image in Book 2–5 episodes.
